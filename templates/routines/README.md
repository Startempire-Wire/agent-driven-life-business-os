# SOVOS Starter Routine Template Library

**Status:** portable starter pack; templates are suggestions, not assignments or authority.

These machine-readable templates implement `operator.routine_template.v1` and are intentionally owner-neutral.

They were seeded from recurring patterns found across the current SOVOS/ADLBOS operating doctrine and the owner's real audit/routine matrices. They are **not** claims that every life or business needs every routine.

The audit/Portfolio Business Compiler must:

1. establish an evidence basis;
2. match one or more templates;
3. show why each template matches;
4. bind real owner/business/life sources into an owner-specific Routine Blueprint;
5. determinize stable steps;
6. obtain required acceptance/authority;
7. compile an exact Routine Instance;
8. shadow/pilot before steady activation.

## Important boundary

A template's `archetype_tags` are **composition hints, not human identities**.

One person/business can match several packs. Template matching may also return `novel_pattern` when the right routine does not yet exist in this library.
