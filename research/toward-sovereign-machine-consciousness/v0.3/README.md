# Toward Sovereign Machine Consciousness — Research Manuscript v0.3

**Status:** DRAFT research preprint and evaluation protocol, dated 22 November 2026. This is a new research publication artifact, **not** an amendment to SOVOS constitutional or product architecture.

The research question is whether persistent agentic systems with source-qualified feedback, goal-directed ad hoc operation, governed Workstreams, a truthful capability self-model, and evidence-linked outcomes perform better than controlled alternatives under equal resources and authority constraints. The work does not report measured results or establish subjective consciousness.

Contents:
- [Full paper](paper.md), intended for later typesetting and potential arXiv submission
- Three accessible vector diagrams in `figures/`: architecture ownership, progressive formalization, pre-registered ablation design matrix

**Evidence boundary:** the paper cites pin-revision public SOVOS/Focusa/UIAI sources and current issue contracts. Wirebot Core/App private design was source-reviewed but is not represented as publicly reproducible. The independent IIT 4.0 finite-model experiment is proposed, not computed. Its operational interaction quantity is not IIT's Phi.

**No software mutation:** this research contribution creates no new runtime, memory/authority store, scheduled operation, owner delegation, machine capability, model, or publication authority. Implementation is owned by the existing product repositories and approved contracts. Scientific publication decisions remain the author's.
