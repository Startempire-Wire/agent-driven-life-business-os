## Read-only first pass

- Audit selected CRM sources with complete pagination, timestamped counts, property definitions and exclusions. Flag automated senders, contractors/vendors, out-of-market sources, clients with open disputes, duplicates, opt-outs and unknown identity; do not treat all contacts as leads.
- Review authorized message history, historical offers, commitments and relevant customer context. Do not infer interest from being in an address book.
- Build source-backed account candidates with **why this account fits**, evidence/freshness, desired offer, possible buying authority, uncertainty, relationship sensitivity, source account and next human/agent action. Where proof is missing, say unknown.
- Deduplicate across CRM accounts by references with confidence and reversible links. Do not merge, copy or overwrite contacts between businesses by default. Each CRM remains authoritative for its own records.

## Deterministic commercial loop

1. **Discover and qualify:** bounded OPP-01 opportunity scouting and TP-CRM-01 reconciliation against legitimate sources and owner-selected market/offer.
2. **Prepare:** source-grounded outreach with approved terms and relevance. Verify suppression, jurisdiction and channel rules. Default output is a reviewable draft, not a send.
3. **Contact only within authority:** existing provider-native send operation when a valid exact scoped grant and send policy allow it; otherwise Needs You. No mass outreach, misleading personalization, fabricated testimonials, hidden list imports or automatic recontact after opt-out.
4. **Respond and follow up:** event-first inbox/CRM observation, deduped run receipts, appropriately timed bounded follow-up and periodic missed-event reconciliation. Follow-Up keeps contact momentum; Follow-Through owns the desired commercial outcome.
5. **Progress:** change CRM stage only on relevant source evidence and applicable write grant. A proposal, meeting, or closed-won property is not a settled payment.
6. **Offer and terms:** resolve the exact offered product and actual entitlement/readiness. Standard authorized checkout terms are different from new pricing, discounts, commitments and contracts, which remain human-reserved.
7. **Cash and delivery:** verify actual currency and settled amount against the payment/accounting provider; track installment balances separately. Then separately verify customer provisioning, delivery and accepted value before declaring onboarding complete.
8. **Continuity:** maintain retention/referral/expansion candidates without presuming permission. Report truthful commercial funnel outcomes and private leverage to Wirebot/Perpetua; W.I.N.S. is never required.
9. **Quiet Kaizen:** propose bounded changes to qualification, timing and workflow from source-attributed outcomes; do not silently mutate an accepted routine instance or source authority.

## Run admission and failure policy

- Prefer provider APIs/CLI and provider events, with existing OpenClaw Gateway automations or approved provider-native scheduling. UIAI only for a permitted external/legacy system lacking a stronger route. A scheduler launches an accepted routine instance revision; it does not supply authority.
- Every consequential effect requires exact owner/account match, fresh grants, idempotency key, contact suppression, authorized operation, receipt and source-side verification. On ambiguous send/payment completion, **re-read provider state before retry**.
- Missed or overlapping runs reconcile; duplicate events coalesce. On provider failure, stale state, wrong tenant, unsupported offer or missing grant: stop the effect, preserve source refs, surface Needs You and the next safe check.
- Do not transfer credentials, raw prospect lists or message bodies into a portable profile, GitHub repository, another tenant or an agent's general memory.
