## One operating concern; one owner

A strategic adviser may design **what** a client should own, automate, delegate, integrate, or stop doing. The client/customer owner retains architecture authority and grants any actual SOVOS agent authority explicitly under the existing owner constitution. A paid founder-led advisory engagement does **not** itself grant agent credentials, administrative privileges, deployment consent or billing permissions.

## Advisory engagement boundary

A complete **decision package** may include business/dependency map, priority recommendation, build/buy/integrate/delegate decisions, owner/agent authority map, implementation owner, cost assumptions, risks, and acceptance evidence. It is a **design and decision artifact**. The actual SOVOS installation or workforce deployment is separately scoped, performed by its authorized owner/delivery team, and evidenced through existing systems.

**Founder capacity constraint:** keep founder-led engagements rare, one active at a time as a *planning assumption*, and measure total effort including sales, travel, preparation, decision work, revisions and aftercare. Product and book creation remain the primary scalable activity. These operating constraints are not encoded as a universal SOVOS deployment limit for other customers.
