## 1. Governing intent

**Income follows assets. Freedom requires stewardship of productive, resilient and lawful assets rather than an endless owner-hour-for-income loop.** This applies to the owner's **whole stewarded environment**, not just a portfolio of revenue products: property, equipment, software, IP, rights, life systems, infrastructure, contracts, obligations and appropriately consented relationship/network context.

Everything may be **evaluated through** an asset/liability/dependency lens. Do not assert that every thing is legally owned, a balance-sheet asset, transferable property, taxable wealth, monetizable, or an obligation. People are never property. One item can be useful, require maintenance, expose one obligation, carry an uncertain value and depend on other assets simultaneously. These are **facets** over stable underlying source-qualified identities, not a binary classification or duplicate records.

### Distinct values that MUST NOT be combined by convenience

1. **Current qualified financial position:** attributable current-value assets **minus actual liabilities** under stated entity/currency/as-of/coverage; partial totals are labeled partial. Cash/bank balances are not the whole net worth.
2. **Illiquid economic equity:** bounded potential market value of private business interests, software/IP or property where rights and comparable/earnings evidence support estimates. Never add stand-alone IP already included in enterprise valuation; stake interests are not the full company's assets.
3. **Productive capacity:** actual qualified business cash collected, contribution margin, recurring delivery economics, asset reuse and owner-hour dependence; forecast pipeline is not current revenue.
4. **Network/social capital:** owner-approved reciprocal relationships, support, distribution permission, referrals and realized business introductions; cannot be converted into owned human property or automatic dollar wealth.
5. **Freedom/lived resilience:** protected time, financial breathing room, reliability, available alternatives, life-enrichment and actual obligations; owner chooses how recovered capacity is spent.

One financial dollar number may be legitimately **negative** while software and intellectual property remain meaningful potential. Never forge positivity by adding unsupported valuation assumptions to signed liabilities. Every estimate carries basis, horizon, currency, bound, source, legal interest, confidence and unknown coverage. Probabilities differ from confidence in the assessment.

A record must have **source, source revision, scope, observation time, coverage, rights/custody evidence, privacy class, correction lifecycle** and an explicit unknown/failure disposition.

```text
   ↓

   ↓
Existing Focusa Workstream / actual granted effect / UIAI or native provider
   ↓
Independent source and receiver outcome / real cash or protection verification
   ↓
Private financial-position / net-leverage / protection / freedom read projections
   ↓
Master Observer & Perpetua: verified betterment, proposal, held result or quiet
   ↘ existing approved memory, correction and authorized next opportunity
```

Only relevant pieces run for a given situation. Discovery cannot activate a costly indefinite campaign; a financial source being disconnected does not block safe physical inventory; lack of OSINT feed does not block manually accepted stewardship of a book; a UI outage does not block the governed private loop. An unqualified consequence path **stops at candidate/proposal** rather than inventing an effect.

## 4. Mandatory decision law for prioritizing

Use the existing Freedom & Leverage **Capacity, Economic and Life Enrichment Frontiers**. A decision compares material source-backed change in owner-defined outcome, risk/fragility, real obligations, legal permission, cash/time/tool/oversight cost, reversibility and shared dependencies. Avoid double counting one shared server/service/reputation benefit across several business items.

**Legal value:** cash or valuation estimate does not by itself authorize liquidation, asset purchase, protected content publication, client communication, debt modification, title transfer or external AI disclosure.

**Threat intelligence:** public advisory, verified affected version, high-likelihood hypothesis, actual incident, and existing debt are different. EPSS/CVSS or vendor severity is not the owner's probability of compromise; match the exact real asset/version/model/contract/horizon, current controls and event freshness before alarm or claim. Use least-data source queries; do not leak private inventory or locations to open web endpoints. Do not launch broad surveillance/active scanning of third-party property.

**Owner capacity:** healthy quiet is acceptable if no action has positive expected net usefulness. A stopped watcher, deleted wasteful routine, safeguarded afternoon or protected rest is not counted as lack of productivity.
