## Core point

The Agent-Driven Life & Business OS should not be marketed as "replacing employees."

A stronger and more defensible comparison is:

> **How much recurring human labor would it take to perform the same modeled business routines?**

A representative ADLBOS routine portfolio can include inbox review, scheduling, meeting follow-up, CRM hygiene, customer inquiry triage, bookkeeping preparation, research, project coordination, reporting, workforce follow-up, system monitoring, routine analytics and ongoing process optimization.

## Reference scenario

The canonical model currently assumes:

```text
50 modeled human hours/week
2,600 human hours/year
mixed administrative + specialist work
```

Current benchmark result:

| Comparison | Approximate annual value |
|---|---:|
| Loaded U.S. employee-equivalent labor | **$133,000/year** |
| Published freelance/contractor equivalent | **$56,082–$104,910/year** |

These are **human-equivalent labor benchmarks**, not ADLBOS pricing or guaranteed savings.

## Simple example

A one-hour-per-business-day inbox routine represents:

## What makes the system different from simply hiring hours

ADLBOS can combine:

- deterministic programs for stable steps;
- OpenClaw durable scheduling;
- Focusa-governed assignments and Evidence;
- UIAI computer/browser execution;
- human-reserved decisions;
- routine analytics;
- owner-attention measurement;
- leverage/momentum inference;
- Quiet Kaizen continuous improvement.

The long-term target is not "more automated activity."

It is:

```text
better outcome
+ less recurring burden
+ more reliable execution
+ reusable capability
+ more owner capacity
+ next higher-leverage opportunity
```

## Website-safe language

### Short

> A representative portfolio of recurring business-support routines can add up to around 50 hours of human work per week. Using current U.S. compensation and freelance-market benchmarks, the modeled workload represents roughly $56,082 to $133,212+ per year of human-equivalent labor, depending on how the work is staffed.

### More precise

> In our reference model, 50 hours per week of mixed administrative, customer, finance, research, project and process-improvement work represents about $133,000 per year of loaded U.S. employee-equivalent labor. Current published freelance/contractor category bands and named proxies put the same modeled workload at roughly $56,082–$104,910 per year. These are workload-equivalent benchmarks—not claims that software replaces employees or guarantees savings.

### Executive-assistant example

> Even a single one-hour-per-business-day executive inbox routine represents roughly $10,000–$14,000 per year of human labor at current executive-assistant and advanced-VA benchmarks.

## Mandatory disclaimer

Use this with every public comparison:

> **Human-equivalent cost estimates are modeled reference values based on stated routine-hour assumptions and current published labor-market data. Actual staffing needs, rates, quality, outcomes and appropriate human involvement vary. The comparison does not claim that ADLBOS replaces an employee or guarantees a specific savings amount.**

## Do not publish

- a sum of several full-time salaries as if all roles would otherwise be hired;
- a claim that one AI worker equals one employee;
- unmeasured customer time savings;
- unverified revenue uplift;
- a fabricated "retail value" or MSRP;
- the actual ADLBOS commercial price unless separately approved.

## Source hierarchy

1. U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational wages.
2. U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Employer Costs for Employee Compensation.
3. Published Upwork functional rate bands.
4. Fiverr only as a secondary marketplace cross-check.
5. Real ADLBOS deployment measurements when available, kept separate from modeled benchmarks.
