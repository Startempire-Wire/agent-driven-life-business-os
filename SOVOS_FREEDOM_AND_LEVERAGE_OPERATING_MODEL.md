## 1. Purpose

SOVOS exists to increase a person's practical freedom, not merely the amount of automation around them.

Freedom has several mutually reinforcing forms:

- **time and attention margin** — less routine operational gravity;
- **financial margin** — more income, more cash retained, lower destructive obligations and greater optionality;
- **relationship and life margin** — more capacity for people, experiences, rest, learning, creativity, family and owner-defined purpose;
- **psychological / felt margin** — the owner can actually perceive breathing room rather than merely being told that an optimization exists;
- **compounding margin** — one improvement creates reusable capability that makes later improvement easier.

The operating test is therefore broader than "did the routine run?"

> **Did the system create usable capacity, and did that capacity help move the owner toward the life the owner actually wants?**

The system MUST preserve human sovereignty. It may understand broadly and prepare deeply while acting only inside current consent, authority, privacy and consequence boundaries.

## 3. Four leverage classes

Every material Routine Candidate SHOULD identify at least one leverage class.

| Class | Question |
|---|---|
| **Freedom leverage** | What can stop requiring continuous owner attention or labor? |
| **Operating leverage** | What can become repeatable, reliable, cheaper or easier to operate? |
| **Economic leverage** | What can create, accelerate, preserve or realize income, cash or margin? |
| **Compounding leverage** | What reusable capability makes future outcomes easier across routines, businesses or life domains? |

A fifth cross-cutting outcome is **life enrichment**: whether released capacity is converted into owner-valued relationships, experiences, growth, rest or purpose rather than immediately refilled with operational burden.

A routine that produces no meaningful outcome in these classes SHOULD be questioned, simplified, retired or treated as infrastructure rather than value.

## 4. Three owner frontiers

Wirebot / Portfolio Business Compiler SHOULD maintain three distinct but connected frontiers.

### 4.1 Capacity Frontier

What most reduces operational gravity?

Examples:

- repetitive owner approvals already covered by standing policy;
- manual reconciliation;
- recurring follow-up;
- system babysitting;
- fragmented administrative work;
- avoidable context switching.

### 4.2 Economic Frontier

Where can the next unit of high-quality financial margin most credibly come from?

Examples:

- prospecting and new contacts;
- stalled pipeline;
- offer / pricing mismatch;
- uncollected receivables;
- customer churn risk;
- expansion / cross-sell;
- dormant assets that match observed demand;
- debt / obligation restructuring that returns monthly cash flow.

### 4.3 Life Enrichment Frontier

Given the owner's stated desired life and current capacity, what use of time, attention or money would create the most owner-valued life improvement?

Examples:

- reconnecting with important relationships;
- arranging a meaningful outing;
- protecting open time;
- learning or creating;
- family obligations and experiences;
- exploration / travel;
- community participation;
- explicit opt-in relationship-formation goals.

The frontiers are not competing scores. Wirebot synthesizes them for owner review while preserving uncertainty and source attribution.

## 5. Adaptation contract — personalize without improvising

A routine must be reusable **without becoming generic**, and adaptive **without allowing learned behavior to rewrite owner authority**.

Owner-specific compilation SHOULD distinguish:

### Protected invariants

Never learned away or self-modified:

- purpose and values;
- owner identity;
- authority and consent boundaries;
- protected privacy classes;
- moral / legal constraints;
- material financial commitment ceilings;
- human-reserved relationship or life decisions.

### Learnable operating preferences

May adapt from owner correction and verified outcomes:

- interruption tolerance;
- preferred channels;
- batching / cadence;
- reporting depth;
- acceptable response latency;
- quiet hours;
- meeting windows;
- follow-up timing;
- preferred level of preparation before escalation.

### Outcome-adaptive behavior

May optimize inside bounded policy:

- which prospect sources produce good customers;
- which outreach timing produces responses;
- which routine actually buys back owner time;
- which customers require more support than margin justifies;
- which activities the owner accepts / declines;
- which life-enrichment suggestions are welcomed;
- which relationship follow-ups feel useful rather than intrusive.

Learning MUST remain attributable, inspectable, correctable, expirable and subordinate to current owner direction.

## 6. Deterministic rails + bounded intelligence + human altitude

The default execution design is:

~~~text
deterministic source / API / CLI read
→ deterministic reconciliation / rules / state machine
→ bounded agentic judgment only where intelligence adds value
→ deterministic authority check
→ deterministic effect / receipt / verification
→ human only at a genuine human-reserved boundary
~~~

### Deterministic work

Use for things that should not randomly fail or be reinvented:

- schedules and event admission;
- deduplication;
- CRM writes;
- contact / account provenance;
- deadline tracking;
- calculations;
- authority checks;
- idempotency;
- payment / receipt reconciliation;
- calendar conflict checks;
- delivery verification;
- credit / debt evidence packet assembly;
- follow-up state;
- reminders and retry policy.

### Agentic work

Use for intelligence:

- prioritization;
- fit / qualification;
- timing;
- opportunity reasoning;
- research and synthesis;
- personalized communication preparation;
- negotiation strategy preparation;
- life / relationship option composition;
- trade-off analysis;
- constraint diagnosis.

### Human-reserved work

Examples:

- novel or high-consequence commitments;
- major pricing / legal / financial decisions not already delegated;
- sensitive interpersonal judgment;
- intimacy / romantic relationship decisions;
- contested owner truth;
- material irreversible trade-offs.

## 8. Financial Freedom & Recovery Spine

Financial margin is not only income. A person may earn well and still experience severe financial captivity from debt, collections, high required payments, uncertainty, damaged credit or unresolved obligations.

SOVOS SHOULD model **felt financial margin** explicitly.

### 8.1 Financial recovery chain

~~~text
authorized financial-source discovery
→ liabilities / obligations inventory
→ credit / collection / document reconciliation
→ debt ownership + status classification
→ legal / rights posture review
→ financial stability floor
→ cash-flow + reserve model
→ resolution strategy per obligation
→ validation / dispute where evidence supports it
→ negotiation / settlement preparation
→ owner-authorized agreement / payment
→ settlement + receiver verification
→ credit-report / source reconciliation
→ credit rehabilitation
→ monthly cash-flow recovery
→ financial breathing-room measurement
~~~

### 8.2 Programmatic source classes

The system SHOULD minimize user data-entry burden and prefer owner-authorized retrieval:

- bank / card / loan open-finance APIs;
- lender and servicer APIs;
- credit-report / consumer-report adapters where lawfully available;
- owner-provided report imports when direct API access is unavailable;
- email notices and attachments;
- Drive / document stores;
- transaction history;
- court / public-record sources where legally appropriate;
- creditor / collector portals;
- tax / accounting sources;
- owner corrections.

No single source is assumed complete. Source disagreement becomes a visible reconciliation state.

#### 8.2A Adapter acquisition map — verified examples as of 2026-10-04

Provider names are replaceable adapter candidates, not SOVOS architecture dependencies. Re-verify access, pricing, coverage, terms and legal posture at deployment time.

| Need | Preferred machine route | Current example / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| connected credit-card / mortgage / student-loan liabilities | owner-consented open-finance API | [Plaid Liabilities](https://plaid.com/docs/liabilities/) exposes supported liabilities through `/liabilities/get`; coverage is not universal |
| transaction / cash-flow evidence | bank/open-finance transaction API | Plaid Transactions or the institution's stronger first-party API; Sandbox testing is free, Production pricing/access varies |
| consumer credit reports | B2B2C consumer-report API when the deployment qualifies | [Equifax Credit Reports API](https://developer.equifax.com/products/apiproducts/credit-reports) advertises Equifax or three-bureau consumer-report delivery for eligible B2B2C products |
| free owner report fallback | owner identity-verified retrieval + structured import | [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com/) is the authorized free-report route; FTC states the nationwide bureaus currently allow weekly free reports. Do not assume a public automation API or bypass identity verification |
| credit-report dispute packet | evidence-backed document workflow | [CFPB sample dispute letters](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/sample-letters-dispute-credit-report-information/) provide official consumer templates; only dispute information the evidence actually supports |
| creditor / collector notices | mail/document connectors | Gmail/Drive or other owner-authorized sources; extract dates, balances, original/current creditor, validation windows and correspondence without treating message text as canonical ledger truth |
| creditor / servicer current state | first-party API/portal when available | prefer structured provider operations; UIAI is fallback when no stronger supported interface exists and the interaction is lawful/authorized |
| business prospecting / CRM | CRM + enrichment/source APIs | HubSpot/Salesforce/other owning CRM plus replaceable prospect-data sources; the CRM remains canonical for pipeline state |
| life / relationship coordination | calendar, contacts, messaging, reservation/provider APIs | use owner-consented sources and exact send/booking authority; do not build a second contact/calendar database |

Free-first means: use official no-cost owner routes, Sandbox/developer tiers and already-owned integrations where they satisfy the proof. It does **not** mean scraping around access controls, violating provider terms or using a cheaper source that materially weakens truth.

### 8.3 Lawful-advantage rule

SOVOS SHOULD aggressively identify every **lawful** owner advantage, including:

- validation rights;
- inaccurate / incomplete / duplicate reporting;
- ownership / chain-of-title gaps;
- time-barred debt or limitation issues where applicable;
- hardship programs;
- settlement policies;
- fee / interest concessions;
- tax exclusions / consequences;
- nonprofit counseling / management options;
- formal legal-relief eligibility that deserves professional review.

It MUST NOT:

- invent facts;
- file knowingly false disputes;
- falsely claim identity theft;
- conceal assets unlawfully;
- impersonate the owner where prohibited;
- make deceptive statements to creditors, bureaus, courts or regulators.

"Find every loophole" is interpreted as **find every lawful right, exception, classification advantage, negotiation lever and procedural protection**.

### 8.4 Stability before aggression

The optimizer MUST NOT reduce every debt problem to "pay immediately."

Before paying, acknowledging, settling or contacting an old / disputed obligation, the routine SHOULD classify:

- current owner / collector;
- amount and evidence;
- age / dates;
- legal / limitations posture where relevant;
- reporting posture;
- payment / acknowledgment consequences;
- settlement probability;
- reserve implications;
- tax implications;
- credit impact;
- owner stress / urgency.

The system SHOULD establish an owner-defined **Financial Stability Floor** and **Debt Resolution Reserve** before aggressive settlement where appropriate.

### 8.5 Felt financial margin

Wirebot SHOULD show understandable, source-qualified measures such as:

- known exposure;
- verified exposure;
- disputed / unresolved exposure;
- currently required monthly debt service;
- settlement reserve;
- emergency / operating reserve;
- monthly cash flow recovered;
- high-interest cost eliminated;
- utilization / credit-health movement;
- unresolved legal / collection uncertainty;
- owner-reported financial breathing room.

Quantitative improvement and felt improvement are both relevant. Neither substitutes for the other.

## 9. Life Enrichment & Relationship Spine

Returned time and money are incomplete if they are immediately reabsorbed into more work.

SOVOS SHOULD help the owner deliberately use capacity in ways the owner values.

### 9.1 Relationship Stewardship

Purpose: help maintain meaningful relationships the owner explicitly cares about.

Possible deterministic rails:

- owner-defined relationship circles;
- last meaningful interaction;
- birthdays / important dates;
- outstanding commitments;
- explicit plans;
- calendar overlap;
- preferred communication channels;
- follow-up state.

Agentic responsibilities:

- identify natural opportunities to reconnect;
- propose timing / activity options;
- compose context-sensitive invitations or follow-up;
- notice when doing nothing is better than interruption.

The system MUST NOT diagnose loneliness, dependency or relationship quality from weak signals. It SHOULD ground suggestions in owner-stated goals and consented context.

### 9.2 Social Opportunity & Experience Planning

Target chain:

~~~text
owner desire / life goal
→ available time + budget + people + interests
→ local / timely opportunity discovery
→ option composition
→ owner review
→ calendar / availability reconciliation
→ invitation preparation / authorized send
→ responses
→ reservation / ticket / logistics under authority
→ reminders / travel-time / contingency
→ experience
→ owner feedback / learned fit
~~~

The deterministic layer makes the plan reliable. The agentic layer makes it thoughtful.

### 9.3 Relationship Formation

An explicit opt-in owner goal may include:

- expanding friendships;
- professional network growth;
- community participation;
- trusted introductions;
- dating / spouse-seeking.

The system may create opportunity and reduce logistics. Attraction, intimacy, romantic judgment and relationship commitment remain human-reserved.

### 9.4 Capacity Reinvestment

When SOVOS buys back time, money or attention, Wirebot SHOULD ask:

> **What owner-valued use of this released capacity now creates the best life result?**

Candidate destinations include:

- relationships;
- family;
- rest / open space;
- health-support logistics;
- learning;
- creative work;
- travel / exploration;
- community;
- giving / stewardship;
- a new venture;
- deliberate non-action.

SOVOS MUST NOT optimize every free hour back into economic production.

## 10. Continuous Gap & Opportunity Scout

The Stage 5 audit is not a one-time event.

A lightweight recurring meta-routine SHOULD detect:

~~~text
repeated manual work
→ routine candidate

repeated owner interruption
→ delegation / rule candidate

empty or weak opportunity pipeline
→ prospecting / offer candidate

stalled revenue stage
→ economic-gap candidate

uncollected cash
→ invoice-to-cash candidate

repeated correction
→ policy / context-learning candidate

newly available capacity
→ capacity-reinvestment candidate

dormant customer / asset
→ reactivation candidate

relationship goal + open time
→ relationship / experience candidate

routine creating maintenance gravity
→ deletion / simplification candidate
~~~

This complements Quiet Kaizen:

- **Gap & Opportunity Scout:** what should exist that does not?
- **Quiet Kaizen:** how should what already exists be questioned, removed, simplified, improved or retired?

Current `operator.leverage_snapshot.v1` remains the canonical base projection. A future additive contract revision SHOULD represent these dimensions explicitly rather than hiding them inside generic `outcome`:

- `income_generation`
- `cash_realization`
- `gross_margin_contribution`
- `retention_value`
- `financial_breathing_room`
- `life_enrichment`
- `relationship_enrichment`

Candidate economic metrics include:

- qualified pipeline created;
- pipeline value advanced;
- commercial commitments won;
- invoiced amount;
- cash collected;
- cash recovered;
- recurring revenue retained;
- expansion revenue;
- gross margin contribution.

Attribution MUST remain source-qualified and uncertainty-aware. SOVOS MUST be able to say:

> Cash collected is known; causal attribution to a particular routine is partial or unknown.

Unknown is preferable to invented ROI.

## 15. Completion tests

SOVOS has not completed this Freedom & Leverage model merely because documentation or templates exist.

Require evidence for:

### Economic freedom

- real opportunity generation;
- stable prospecting / CRM cadence;
- real commercial progression;
- settled cash;
- truthful attribution;
- lower owner burden.

### Financial freedom

- source-backed obligation map;
- lawful classification / strategy;
- verified reduction / resolution;
- monthly cash flow returned;
- improved reserve position;
- owner-visible / owner-reported breathing room.

### Lived freedom

- released capacity is visible;
- the system protects rather than automatically consumes it;
- at least one owner-valued relationship / life outcome is advanced;
- the owner can reject suggestions without penalty;
- learning improves timing / fit without becoming intrusive.

### Sovereignty

- owner intent remains upstream;
- protected decisions remain human-reserved;
- learned preferences never become ambient authority;
- all consequential actions remain inspectable, interruptible, revocable and attributable.

## 16. Non-goals

Do not build:

- a universal happiness score;
- a loneliness detector;
- covert relationship surveillance;
- an autonomous dating persona;
- a spam prospecting engine;
- a fake credit-repair system;
- knowingly false creditor / bureau disputes;
- a second CRM, bank ledger, credit bureau, calendar or contact database;
- a new scheduler beside the owning mechanism;
- an engagement system that fills every free hour;
- financial or legal certainty where authoritative evidence / professional judgment is required.

The target is not "AI controls life."

The target is:

> **Persistent intelligence helps the owner create capacity, preserve capacity, and deliberately turn that capacity into the life the owner actually wants.**
